Ingredient Deep Dive

AG1 contains 75 ingredients across four main complexes. Here's what actually matters:

Vitamin & Mineral Blend

Covers 100%+ DV of vitamins A, C, E, B-complex, zinc, selenium, chromium, and more. Uses bioavailable forms (methylated B vitamins, chelated minerals). This alone replaces a quality multivitamin worth $15-30/month.

Superfood Complex

Spirulina, chlorella, wheat grass, barley grass, broccoli flower, and more. Good doses of phytonutrients and antioxidants. These are where the "greens" come from.

Probiotic Strain (7.2 Billion CFU)

Lactobacillus acidophilus UALa-01 at 7.2 billion CFU. Decent but not exceptional — dedicated probiotics offer 10-50 billion CFU with multiple strains. Still, it's a solid baseline.

Adaptogen & Mushroom Blend

Ashwagandha, rhodiola, reishi, shiitake. Adaptogens support stress response and energy. The doses are moderate — not as high as dedicated adaptogen supplements, but meaningful.

The Proprietary Blend Issue

AG1 uses proprietary blends, meaning they disclose the total weight of each complex but not the exact dose of every individual ingredient. This is the biggest criticism from the supplement science community, and it's valid. However, AG1 has been more transparent than most, publishing some individual ingredient amounts and third-party test results.

Our Ingredient Verdict

AG1's formula is genuinely comprehensive. It won't replace a high-dose therapeutic supplement for a specific deficiency, but as a daily nutritional foundation, it covers more bases than any other single product we've tested. The quality of ingredient forms (bioavailable, chelated) is notably above average.

Our 6-Month Experience

Both testers noticed improved digestion within the first week — likely from the probiotics and digestive enzymes. One tester experienced mild bloating for days 2-4 that resolved on its own (common with greens powders as gut bacteria adjust). By week 2, morning energy felt noticeably more consistent without the usual mid-morning crash.

Months 2-3: Consistent Benefits

The energy improvement solidified. Neither tester was reaching for afternoon coffee anymore. Subjective mood and focus felt marginally better, though it's hard to isolate AG1 from other lifestyle factors. Skin quality improved for one tester — clearer, more even tone. Digestion remained excellent for both.

Months 4-6: Long-Term & Bloodwork

Both testers got bloodwork at month 0 and month 6. Key findings:

  • B12 levels increased to optimal range for both

  • Iron, zinc, and magnesium all within optimal range

We can't attribute all improvements solely to AG1 (other lifestyle factors exist), but the correlation with filling micronutrient gaps is clear.

The Compliance Factor

This is AG1's secret weapon. Taking one scoop in water every morning is infinitely easier than managing 5-8 separate supplement bottles. Our compliance rate was 98% over 6 months. Previous supplement routines with multiple pills had averaged 60-70% compliance. The best supplement is the one you actually take consistently.

Taste & Mixability

AG1 tastes like a mild, slightly sweet tropical greens juice. It's not delicious, but it's genuinely pleasant — far better than most greens powders. On a 1-10 taste scale, we'd give it a 7. It mixes well in a shaker bottle with cold water (no clumps), and tastes even better blended with ice.

Pro tip: Mix with cold water and a squeeze of lemon. It tastes like a light lemonade-greens fusion. Don't mix with warm water — it activates the enzymes and tastes grassy.
